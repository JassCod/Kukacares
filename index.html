About Kuka Cares

We see a world where every person lives to their fullest potential — beyond barriers and limitations.

01

Our Vision

A world where people with disability live to their fullest potential, beyond barriers and limitations.

02

Our Mission

To empower participants with support and services that build skills, confidence and independence, at home and in the community.

03

Our Promise

Your plan, your goals, your choices. We listen first, then shape every shift of support around the life you want to live.

What we do

Support for every part of your day.

From your morning routine to clinical care and getting out into your community, our NDIS services are flexible, reliable and designed around you.

Daily Living Support

Help with personal hygiene, meal preparation, medication management, household tasks and transport, so home life runs smoothly.

  • Personal care
  • Meals
  • Medication
  • Transport

Daily Care

Consistent, respectful day-to-day care that builds routines, protects dignity and helps you keep doing more for yourself.

  • Routines
  • Independence
  • Wellbeing

Nursing Care

Qualified nurses providing clinical care at home, including wound care, catheter care, vital-signs monitoring and complex health support.

  • Wound care
  • Catheter care
  • Vital signs

NDIS Support

We help you understand and navigate the NDIS, make the most of your plan and connect with the right supports for your goals.

  • Plan guidance
  • Goal setting

Community Access

Get out and about with confidence: social outings, recreation, appointments, shopping and the activities that matter to you.

  • Social
  • Recreation
  • Skills

In-Home Support

Trusted support workers who come to you, helping you live a more independent and fulfilling life in your own home.

  • At home
  • Flexible hours

Our values

Six promises we live by.

Every decision, every visit and every conversation is guided by them.

01

Quality

We continually improve our service standards so the care you receive is consistently excellent.

02

Autonomy

You lead. We support participants to make their own decisions about their life and their care.

03

Integrity

We act honestly, ethically and transparently, doing what we say we will do.

04

Inclusiveness

We respect and celebrate diversity in culture, ability, identity and background.

05

Empathy

We take the time to understand each participant's circumstances, feelings and goals.

06

Accountability

We own our decisions and outcomes, and we are always open to feedback.
